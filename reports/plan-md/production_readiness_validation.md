# Production Readiness Validation

## Readiness model

Production readiness combines deployment readiness, operational readiness, and team readiness.

## Gatekeeper integration

- Kubernetes without observability raises an engineering readiness warning.
- Microservices raise a DevOps baseline and platform maturity warning.
- AI surfaces without monitoring raise an engineering readiness warning.

## UI validation

The frontend panel includes production readiness, maturity rings, risk summaries, offline state, and delivery complexity radar coverage.
