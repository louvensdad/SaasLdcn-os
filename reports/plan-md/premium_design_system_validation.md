- Surfaces: primary, secondary, tertiary, accent.
- Shadows: reduced opacity and lift.

Validation passed in the production build and in responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile screenshots. Theme-aware localization selects passed in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.
