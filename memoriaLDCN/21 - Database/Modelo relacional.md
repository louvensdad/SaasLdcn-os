# Modelo relacional

Users pertencem a Organizations; Organizations possuem Workspaces; Workspaces possuem Projects. Projects possuem Files, Versions, Builds, Containers, Deploys, Memories, Conversations e Automations.

AgentRuns vinculam Agents a Projects, Versions e Builds. Tokens e Billing pertencem ao Workspace e registram consumo e cobrança.
